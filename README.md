## 🚀 Projects
 
### 📈 Data Science & Analysis
 
**[The Economic Aftershocks — Earthquake Impact on National Recovery](https://github.com/betlm/DSA210_project)** `DSA 210 · Introduction to Data Science · Sabancı University`
 
Merged three heterogeneous datasets (EM-DAT, World Bank API, USGS) covering 959 earthquake events across 110 countries. Applied non-parametric hypothesis testing (Spearman, Kruskal-Wallis, Mann-Whitney U) and built Random Forest & Logistic Regression classifiers for economic recovery prediction — achieving a **20.8 pp F1-score improvement** over baseline.

Built a competitive multiplayer word guessing game inspired by the Turkish game Parolla. Two players race head-to-head in real time, guessing words from definitions across 15 rounds with live scoreboards, a pass queue, and an early-finish decision flow — all powered by WebSockets. Integrates every major layer of CS50x: Python/Flask backend, SQLite database, and a dynamic vanilla JS frontend.
